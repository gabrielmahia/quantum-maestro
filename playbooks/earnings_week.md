# Playbook: Heavy Earnings Cluster (mega-cap week)
**Rules in force:** EVENT-BLACKOUT per-underlying. No short premium on a name inside 24h of its print.
**Do:** run the seven-agent stack on paper against the biggest print of the week and grade it after - free out-of-sample test with a known answer in 72h.
**Don't:** hold defined-risk directional spreads through a print at full size; confuse a good company with a good trade.
**Note:** index exposure carries single-name event risk when leadership is concentrated - check breadth before assuming SPY diversifies you.
