# Playbook: CPI / PPI / NFP Morning
**Rules in force:** EVENT-BLACKOUT for short premium. No new positions between 8:25-9:45 ET.
**Do:** note the expected move priced by the straddle the day before; grade afterward whether vol was rich or cheap - this calibrates the Options Agent.
**Don't:** hold overnight short premium into the print unless entered >24h prior at DEFENSIVE size.
**Pattern to respect:** the first reaction frequently retraces; institutions trade the revision details, not the headline.
