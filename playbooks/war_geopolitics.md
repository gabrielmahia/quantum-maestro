# Playbook: Geopolitical Escalation (oil/shipping-relevant conflict)
**Trigger:** shooting-war escalation touching energy supply, straits, or reserve currencies. Sets geopolitical_flag=True -> regime engine typically outputs DEFENSIVE.
**Do:** separate verified facts / interpretation / speculation before acting; cut size to 0.3x; prefer cash; hedges permitted; watch oil, gold, USD, and credit - not social media.
**Don't:** predict ("market crashes Monday"); short into panic lows; sell vol because "it always comes back."
